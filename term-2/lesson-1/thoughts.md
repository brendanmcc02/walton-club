# Thoughts after teaching the class
* the idea before teaching the class was to drip-feed the kids the project, so they'd slowly/incrementally work on the project throughout term 1 + 2
* on paper i thought it would work well, but now (19th Feb), I'm not so sure
* they can't really work on the project without learning the necessary skills first - pandas and matplotlib

# if i were to teach this class again
* in the first class i could tell them about the project + posters i.e. whats expected by the end of term
    * just to give them the heads up and have awareness about the end-goal
    * this also adds more purpose to the teaching - "this material is relevant"
* but imo for lesson 1 don't bother with:
    1. putting people into groups
    2. asking them to pick an area of interest
    3. making a research question
    4. finding datasets
* they haven't worked with this stuff, so they frankly have no idea what even a .csv is
* imo they should learn the material first (pandas + matplotlib), then work on projects
* in hindsight, it doesn't make sense how in lesson 1 i formed them into groups and got them thinking about final projects
    * and then suddenly in lesson 2+3+4 we don't talk/think about projects at all

# a decent lesson structure (off the top of my head)
* Lessons 1-4: Pandas & matplotlib
* Lessons 5-8: working on projects
