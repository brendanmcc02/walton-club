# What I learned

* do kahoots in teams
    * energy is higher and it has collaboration
    * individuals is more boring

* create a very step-by-step structured guide for projects
    * getting started is often the hardest part and kids may feel intimidated
    * if i create a guide like this and hold their hand, they develop the self-confidence from this
    * tbh this would be very similar to the `term-2-lesson-4-worksheet.pdf`, but just need to adapt it more generally to an open-ended project

* i don't think the `code-a-long` idea works well tbh
    * the kids work at different paces and self-learning is the best form of learning
    * give them a physical worksheet (so they can't copy/paste), and let them work through the exercises
    * make it step-by-step and hold their hand, we want to build their confidence

* teach them the skills in lessons 1-4 first (pandas + matplotlib)
* then tell them about the projects
    * it's not worth telling them about datasets and having them look for datasets when they actually have no idea what it really is

## Term 1 Feedback
* exercises were too difficult, make them easier!
    * but add a few exercises at the end for the kids who are very talented
    * in term 1 we had one kid Darshin who knew a lot of python
    * and he was not in term 2, so my guess is that he left TWC because perhaps it was too easy
    * some kids are at different programming levels and we should also cater to the highly-talented ones
* don't go too crazy on making difficult problems
    * what ends up happening is the kids get super discouraged and walk away from the session with a lack of self-confidence
    * we want to build up their skills and confidence, they are not here to learn something for an exam, this is all non-examinable
